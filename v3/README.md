# Model vědomí v3.x

[← Hlavní README](../README.md)

**Aktuální stav:** v3.2, kandidát konsolidace (14. 10. 2026). Navazuje na v3.0 (duben 2026) a pracovní verzi v3.1 (květen 2026).

**English summary.** Version 3.x reframes the model as embodied predictive processing under a replication imperative with an allostatic budget: nine layers (L0–L8) and three cross-cutting principles (P-CMPLX, P-VAL, P-REPL). The v3.2 consolidation adds verified errata, structural contradictions, decisions pending the author's confirmation, a proposed refined core, candidates from recent discussions and a companion synthesis on why brains evolved and where decisions come from. The documents are in Czech.
